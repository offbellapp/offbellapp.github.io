Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Offbell doesn’t create an account and has no server of its own. Your limits and settings stay on your phone. In the free plan, ads are shown by Google AdMob, which collects the data described below.

What stays on your phone

  • Limits and settings: Limit names, lock lengths, days, your language choice and other settings are stored only on this phone and are never sent to us.
  • The apps you choose: You pick apps, categories and websites with iOS’s Screen Time picker. iOS gives Offbell unreadable tokens instead, so Offbell doesn’t learn which apps you chose.
  • PIN: Only a one-way hash of your PIN (SHA-256), not the PIN itself, is stored on this phone.
  • Face ID: When you open Offbell, iOS checks Face ID or your passcode. Offbell only learns whether the check succeeded; it never accesses face data.
  • Notifications: The “locked” and “opened” notifications are created on your phone.

Ads (free plan)

In the free plan, a full-screen ad appears when Offbell opens and a banner sits at the bottom of the screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, run analytics and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ads shown and tapped, and information about how you interact with the app
  • Diagnostic data such as crashes and performance

Offbell doesn’t ask for permission to track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency), so iOS doesn’t give the advertising identifier (IDFA) to Offbell or Google. Offbell doesn’t ask for location access either. To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

With an Offbell subscription, no ads are shown and AdMob doesn’t collect this data.

Subscription and payments

Apple handles payments. Your card and payment details never reach Offbell. Offbell checks whether your subscription is active on your phone using Apple’s StoreKit.

Children

Offbell is made for adults and parents; a parent may install it on their child’s phone. Offbell itself doesn’t collect personal data from children. For ads shown in the free plan, the ads section above applies; a subscription removes the ads.

Deleting your data

Everything Offbell stores is on your phone, so deleting Offbell deletes it too. For advertising data held by Google, use Google’s privacy tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
