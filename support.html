Support

Offbell locks the apps you choose for as long as you choose, then opens them when time is up. While the switch stays on, it locks them again at the same time the next day.

Common questions

Why does Offbell need Screen Time access?

The lock is set and lifted by iPhone’s Screen Time technology. Without access, Offbell only shows a sample flow and the real lock doesn’t work. You can check access in Settings > Screen Time.

The lock didn’t start when I expected.

Check that the limit’s switch is on and that today is one of the selected days. If Screen Time access was removed, the lock can’t work; open Offbell and allow it again.

How do I stop the daily lock?

Turn off the switch next to the limit. If you set a PIN, Offbell asks for it.

How do I use it on my child’s phone?

Install Offbell on your child’s phone, set up the limits and add a 4-digit PIN in Settings. Turning off, changing or deleting a limit asks for this PIN. To keep Offbell from being deleted, turn off app deletion in Content & Privacy Restrictions in the iPhone’s Screen Time settings.

I forgot my PIN.

The PIN is stored on this phone and can’t be recovered. You can delete and reinstall Offbell; your limits are removed too and you’ll need to set them up again.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In your iPhone Settings, under your Apple Account, in Subscriptions. On a new phone, tap “Restore purchases” in Offbell to get your subscription back.

I’d like a refund.

Apple handles payments, so refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
